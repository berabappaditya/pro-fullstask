How to use this book

Read a chapter, look at the picture, say the mnemonic out loud. That's the whole method.

Each chapter follows the same shape so your brain knows where to look. You will meet these boxes again and again:

At Ramu's chai stall

The running story. Ramu starts with one tiny tea stall in Medinipur and grows it into a national chain. Every problem he hits is a real system-design problem in disguise. If you remember the story, you remember the concept.

Definition

The precise, interview-ready meaning in one or two sentences.

In plain words

The same idea explained to someone who has never written code.

Lock it in

A short hook to memorise.

Mnemonics, one-liners and "if you remember only one thing" summaries.

Common trap

Mistakes people make in real systems and in interviews.

Interview tip

What interviewers listen for, and how to phrase your answer.

Code examples are in Java, written as before / after pairs: the red-edged block shows the problem, the green-edged block shows the fix. Comments explain the code line by line.

Blue-bordered panels marked Live are simulators. Click the buttons and watch the idea happen. Tick Mark chapter as done at the end of each chapter; your progress shows in the chapter list on the left and is saved in this browser.
