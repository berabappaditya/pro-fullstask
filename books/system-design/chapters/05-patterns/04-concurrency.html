Concurrency in LLD: when two people grab the last samosa

Many LLD questions (booking seats, parking spots, wallets) are really about concurrency. Interviewers want to see you protect shared state.

At Ramu's chai stall

One samosa left. Two cashiers each check the tray ("yes, one left"), each take money from a customer, each walk to the tray. One customer gets a samosa; the other gets an apology. Checking and taking were two separate steps, and someone squeezed in between. That's a race condition.
