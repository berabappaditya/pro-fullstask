CAP, PACELC and consistency models

When the network between your servers breaks, you must choose: refuse some requests to stay correct, or answer everything and risk being out of date.

At Ramu's chai stall

A storm cuts the phone line between the Kharagpur and Kolkata branches. A customer in Kolkata asks, "How many loyalty points do I have? I want to redeem 100." The Kolkata branch can't check with Kharagpur, where he might have spent points this morning.

Choice 1: "Sorry, our system is down, come back later." Correct but unhelpful. That's choosing Consistency. Choice 2: "Your balance as of last night is 250, enjoy!" Helpful but maybe wrong. That's choosing Availability. He can't have both while the line is cut, and the line cut is the Partition.

C
Consistency

Every read sees the most recent write (or gets an error). All nodes show the same data at the same time.

A
Availability

Every request to a working node gets a non-error response, even if it's not the latest data.

P
Partition tolerance

The system keeps running even when messages between nodes are lost or delayed.

Which to choose?

SystemChoiceWhy
Bank transfer, stock trading, seat bookingCPShowing a wrong balance or selling one seat twice is worse than a short error
Social feed, likes count, product reviewsAPA like count that's 2 seconds old harms nobody; downtime loses users
Shopping cart (Amazon's Dynamo paper)APAlways let people add to cart; merge conflicting carts later
Configuration / leader election (etcd, ZooKeeper)CPTwo nodes both believing they're leader is catastrophic

PACELC: the part CAP forgets

Definition

If Partition: choose A or C. Else (normal operation): choose Latency or Consistency. Even with a healthy network, waiting for replicas to agree makes every request slower.

DynamoDB and Cassandra are PA/EL (available in partitions, fast normally). Traditional SQL with synchronous replicas and Google Spanner are PC/EC (consistent always, paying latency). MongoDB is configurable.

The consistency ladder

Common trap

"We'll pick CA." In a system spread over more than one machine, partitions will happen, so CA isn't a real option. Also, the C in CAP (linearizability) is not the C in ACID (integrity rules). Same letter, different meaning.

Lock it in

Phone line cut? Be Correct or be Kind.

P is forced on you. During a partition choose CP (money, bookings, locks) or AP (feeds, likes, carts). PACELC adds: even without failures, consistency costs latency.

Interview questions

Is Cassandra CP or AP?

By default AP: any replica can accept writes and reads return what that replica has. But it's tunable per query: with QUORUM reads and writes (R + W > N) you get strong consistency for that data, at the cost of availability if too many replicas are unreachable.

For a ticket booking system, which parts are CP and which AP?

Seat reservation and payment are CP: never double-book; it's fine to show "try again". Browsing events, showing approximate seats left, reviews and recommendations are AP: stale by a few seconds is fine, downtime is not. Mixing guarantees per feature is the mature answer.
