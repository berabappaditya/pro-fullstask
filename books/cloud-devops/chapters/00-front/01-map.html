How this book works

Five parts: cloud foundations, running code, containers and delivery, operating in production, and Linux. Every service is shown on AWS and GCP side by side.

Ramu rents kitchens in a food court

Instead of building his own restaurant buildings, Ramu now rents kitchen space in giant food courts run by two landlords, Amazon and Google. He rents as many stoves as today's crowd needs and pays by the hour (the cloud). His recipes travel in sealed tiffin boxes that taste the same in any kitchen (containers). A floor manager decides which kitchen runs which tiffin and replaces any stove that breaks (Kubernetes). A conveyor belt from his test kitchen to every counter delivers new recipes safely (CI/CD).

How deep?

An SDE-2 interview won't ask you to recite every AWS service. It asks you to design and justify: "how would you deploy this service, make it highly available, secure it and know when it breaks?". Each chapter ends with that kind of question. The Spring Boot app we deploy in {{ref:ch-deploy-boot}} is the one built in {{xref:spring-boot/ch-crud}}.
