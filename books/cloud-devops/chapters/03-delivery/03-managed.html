Where to run containers: ECS, Fargate, EKS, GKE or Cloud Run

Same container, different amounts of platform you manage. Choose the simplest option that meets your needs.

In the food court

Ramu can hand his tiffins to the food court's own serving service and never see a stove (Cloud Run, Fargate), or hire a floor manager and run his own counters with full control (EKS/GKE). The first is easier; the second is more flexible.

Interview answer

"For a handful of stateless services I'd start on Cloud Run or ECS Fargate: no cluster to patch, autoscaling built in. Once we have many services, need service mesh, custom operators, GPUs or portability across clouds, a managed Kubernetes (EKS or GKE Autopilot) pays for its complexity."

Interview questions

ECS vs EKS?

ECS is AWS's own simpler orchestrator tightly integrated with AWS (IAM per task, ALB, CloudWatch); EKS is managed Kubernetes with the full open ecosystem (Helm, operators, portability) but more concepts and upgrades to manage. Both run on EC2 nodes or Fargate.
