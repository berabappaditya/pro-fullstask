VPC networking: subnets, routes, NAT and firewalls

A VPC is your private network in the cloud. Public subnets face the internet through an internet gateway; private subnets reach out through NAT; security groups decide which traffic each resource accepts.

In the food court

Ramu's section is fenced off (VPC). The serving counter faces the public hall (public subnet). The kitchen and storeroom are at the back with no public door (private subnets); staff can still go out to buy supplies through a back gate that only opens outward (NAT gateway). Each room has its own bouncer with a guest list (security group).

Interview questions

What makes a subnet public?

Its route table has a route for 0.0.0.0/0 to an internet gateway (and resources have public IPs). Private subnets route 0.0.0.0/0 to a NAT gateway (outbound only) or have no internet route at all.

An app in a private subnet can't reach a third-party API. What do you check?

The subnet's route table (0.0.0.0/0 → NAT gateway in a public subnet, and that NAT's subnet routes to the IGW), the security group's outbound rules, the NACL in both directions (stateless, including ephemeral ports), DNS resolution, and whether the third party allow-lists your NAT's elastic IP.
