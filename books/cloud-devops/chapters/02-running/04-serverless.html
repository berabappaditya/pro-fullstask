Serverless and messaging: Lambda, Cloud Run, queues and events

Serverless runs your code only when something happens and bills per use; queues and topics connect pieces without them waiting on each other.

In the food court

A stove that lights itself when an order slip drops in and switches off when idle: you pay only for cooking time (serverless). The first order after a quiet hour waits a moment while it heats up (cold start). Order slips pile on a spike so the kitchen works at its own pace (queue); an announcement over the loudspeaker reaches every counter that cares (pub/sub).

Interview questions

When would you not use Lambda?

Long-running work (over 15 minutes), steady high traffic (containers are cheaper), latency-critical paths that can't tolerate cold starts, heavy frameworks with slow startup, or when you need persistent connections or local state. Cloud Run/Fargate or Kubernetes fit better.

SQS standard vs FIFO?

Standard: nearly unlimited throughput, at-least-once delivery, best-effort ordering. FIFO: strict ordering within a message group and exactly-once processing within a deduplication window, with lower throughput limits. Choose FIFO only when order truly matters (per account, per order).
