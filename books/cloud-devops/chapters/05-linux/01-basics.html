Interview questions

Find the 10 most frequent errors in a log file.

grep ERROR app.log | awk -F'ERROR' '{print $2}' | sort | uniq -c | sort -rn | head -10: filter error lines, keep the message part, sort so duplicates are adjacent, count, sort by count descending, top 10.

What does chmod 644 mean? 755?

644: owner read/write, group and others read only (typical files). 755: owner full, group and others read and execute (typical directories and scripts).
