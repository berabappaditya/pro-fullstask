Actuator, logging and observability

Actuator exposes health, metrics and runtime info; Micrometer sends metrics and traces to your monitoring stack; structured logs tie it together.

Interview questions

How do you change a log level in production without restarting?

POST to /actuator/loggers/com.ramu.orders with {"configuredLevel":"DEBUG"} (secured endpoint), or via a config server refresh. Revert afterwards.

Liveness vs readiness probes?

Liveness: is the process healthy or should Kubernetes restart it (don't include downstream dependencies, or a DB outage restarts every pod). Readiness: can it take traffic now (warming up, draining during shutdown, critical dependency down); failing it removes the pod from the Service without restarting.
