CompletableFuture: async pipelines without blocking

A Future you can chain: "when this finishes, do that", combine several results, and handle errors, all without a thread sitting and waiting.

In Ramu's kitchen

With a plain Future, the head chef hands out a ticket and then stands at the counter staring until it's ready (get() blocks). With CompletableFuture he leaves a note on the ticket: "when the rice is done, add the dal; when both rice and curry are done, plate the thali; if anything burns, serve plain roti". Then he goes off to do other work.

Common trap

Without an executor argument, supplyAsync runs on ForkJoinPool.commonPool(), shared by the whole JVM including parallel streams. Blocking I/O there starves everything else. Always pass your own I/O pool (or a virtual-thread executor). Also: join() throws unchecked CompletionException; get() throws checked ExecutionException.

Lock it in

Apply transforms, Compose chains, Combine joins, allOf waits.

Start independent work at the same time, pass your own executor, add a timeout and a fallback.

Interview questions

Future vs CompletableFuture?

Future only lets you check or block (get()). CompletableFuture can be completed manually, chained with callbacks, combined with others, and has built-in error handling and timeouts, enabling non-blocking pipelines.

thenApply vs thenApplyAsync?

thenApply runs the function on whichever thread completed the previous stage (or the caller, if already complete). thenApplyAsync submits it to an executor (the common pool, or the one you pass), which is useful when the next step is heavy.
