How this book works

Six parts, from "what is the JVM?" to "why is my server out of memory?" Read in order the first time; jump around when revising.

In Ramu's kitchen

Before Ramu's chai stall became a chain (that's the System Design book), it was one busy kitchen. The kitchen is our running picture for Java. The restaurant building is a process. The chefs are threads. The recipe cards are classes; the dishes made from them are objects. The storeroom shelves are the heap, each chef's personal notepad is their stack, and the cleaner who clears plates nobody uses is the garbage collector.
