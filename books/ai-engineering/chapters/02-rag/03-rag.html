Interview questions

Explain RAG and when you'd use it.

Retrieval-augmented generation retrieves relevant passages from your own knowledge store at query time and includes them in the prompt so the model answers grounded in them. Use it for private, frequently changing or large knowledge (docs, policies, tickets), when you need citations, per-user access control, and lower hallucination, without retraining.

Your RAG bot gives wrong answers. How do you debug?

Separate retrieval from generation: look at the retrieved chunks for failing questions. If the right chunk isn't retrieved: fix ingestion (parsing tables/PDFs), chunking, metadata filters, add hybrid search, query rewriting, reranking, or more k. If it is retrieved but the answer is wrong: improve the prompt (answer only from context, cite), reduce noisy chunks, order context better, or use a stronger model. Track both with an evaluation set.
