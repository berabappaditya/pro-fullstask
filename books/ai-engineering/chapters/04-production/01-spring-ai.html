Interview questions

What is an advisor in Spring AI?

An interceptor around ChatClient calls (like a servlet filter or AOP advice): it can modify the request (add memory, retrieved documents, system rules) and the response (validate, log, retry). Advisors are ordered and composable, which keeps cross-cutting AI concerns out of business code.
