Prompting vs RAG vs fine-tuning

RAG changes what the model knows right now; fine-tuning changes how it behaves. Try prompting first, RAG for knowledge, fine-tuning for style, format or a narrow skill at scale.

Ramu's assistant

To teach the assistant today's prices, Ramu hands him the price list (RAG). To make him write like Ramu's brand, short, warm and in Hinglish, Ramu sends him on a training course with hundreds of examples (fine-tuning). A course won't teach tomorrow's prices.

Interview questions

A team wants to fine-tune a model on their docs so it answers questions about them. What do you advise?

Use RAG instead (or first): fine-tuning doesn't reliably inject facts, can't cite, goes stale on every doc change, and can't respect per-user permissions. Fine-tune later only if evaluation shows a behaviour/format gap that prompting and RAG can't close, or to cut cost with a smaller tuned model.

What is LoRA?

Low-Rank Adaptation: instead of updating all weights, train small low-rank matrices added to certain layers. Far cheaper in compute and storage; many adapters can share one base model. QLoRA does it on a quantised base model to fit on smaller GPUs.
