LLM features fail differently from normal code: silently, probabilistically and expensively. Trace every call, guard inputs and outputs, control cost, and plan for provider outages.

Ramu's assistant

Interview questions

How do you monitor an LLM feature in production?

Technical metrics (latency, errors, 429s, tokens, cost per request/tenant), quality signals (user feedback, escalation rate, groundedness scores from sampled LLM-judge runs, refusal rate), safety signals (guardrail triggers, injection attempts), and traces linking prompt version, retrieved context and tool calls to each answer, with alerts on drift.
