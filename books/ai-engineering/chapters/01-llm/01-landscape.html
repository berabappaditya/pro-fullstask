The AI landscape for engineers

Most product work is not training models; it's composing them: calling a hosted model through an API, giving it the right context, letting it use tools, and measuring the result.

Ramu's assistant

Ramu doesn't raise and educate an assistant from birth (training a model). He hires one from an agency (a model API), gives it a job card (prompt), the company handbook (RAG), a phone (tools), and a supervisor checking its work (evaluation and guardrails).

Interview questions

Hosted API vs self-hosted open-weight model?

Hosted (Claude, GPT, Gemini via vendor or cloud): best quality, no GPU ops, pay per token, data leaves your boundary (mitigated by enterprise terms, cloud-region hosting like Bedrock/Vertex). Self-hosted open-weight (Llama, Mistral, Qwen with vLLM): full data control, predictable cost at high volume, customisation, but you operate GPUs, scaling and upgrades, and quality may trail frontier models.
