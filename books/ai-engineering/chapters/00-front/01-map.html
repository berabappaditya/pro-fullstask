How this book works

Four parts: how LLMs work, retrieval-augmented generation, tools/agents/MCP, and production AI with Spring AI. No maths beyond what you need to reason about behaviour.

Ramu hires an AI assistant

Ramu hires an assistant who has read millions of books but never worked at his chain (an LLM). It writes beautifully but sometimes confidently invents things. So Ramu gives it an open recipe book to look things up before answering (RAG), a phone with speed-dial buttons to check stock and place orders (tool calling), the freedom to work through a task step by step (agents), and one standard socket that any appliance can plug into (MCP).

What SDE-2 interviews ask in 2026

Expect "design a RAG chatbot over our docs", "how do you stop hallucinations?", "how would you evaluate it?", "what is MCP and how is it different from function calling?", "how do you control cost and latency?" and "how do you defend against prompt injection?". The last chapter, {{ref:ch-ai-sd}}, walks a full design answer. Code is Java with Spring AI 2.0 (on Spring Boot 4, see {{xref:spring-boot/ch-boot4}}).
