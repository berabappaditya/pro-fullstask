One story, five books

Ramu's chai business teaches everything

Ramu starts with a tea stall in Medinipur. In each book his business grows a little, and every problem he hits is a real engineering problem in disguise.

Java Core

Ramu's kitchen: chefs are threads, the order board is shared memory, and two chefs grabbing the same pan is a deadlock.

Spring Boot

The franchise kit: head office hires and wires your staff (IoC), and the kit arrives pre-assembled (auto-configuration).

System Design

The national chain: more stalls (scaling), a manager at the gate (load balancer), flasks on the counter (cache).

Cloud & DevOps

Ramu rents kitchens in a food court instead of building his own, and ships a sealed tiffin box (container) to each one.

AI Engineering

Ramu hires an AI assistant that reads his recipe book before answering (RAG) and plugs into his tools through one standard socket (MCP).
